## Description

Commission legal review of wiretap/eavesdropping statutes, COPPA, GDPR children's-data provisions, and app-store monitoring-app policies before any message-content or call-metadata feature ships.

This is a **Legal function** that Engineering cannot complete, but Engineering has scaffolded the compliance-mapping artifact (`docs/compliance/jurisdiction-matrix.md`) for Legal to fill in.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Legal sign-off obtained and documented per target launch jurisdiction
- [ ] Findings mapped to specific PRD features (call/SMS access, location tracking)
- [ ] Any jurisdiction where a feature cannot ship is flagged for regional feature-gating (Engineering will implement flags)
- [ ] App store policies (Google Play, Apple App Store) reviewed and documented for monitoring-app category
